# Portrait brief — halwall.me

**Subject:** Hal Wall (Henry Wall), London.
**Why this brief is specific:** the portrait is not decoration on this site. It sits astride the seam between the two halves of the design, rendered twice from a single file with two different grades. The lighting does the concept's work, so the frame has to be shot for it.

---

## The one thing that matters

**A single hard key from camera-left, with deep falloff across the face.**

The site splits the image down the middle. The left half is graded cold and desaturated (the engineer); the right half is graded warm (the founder of a literature app). If the face is lit flat, both halves look the same and the whole idea collapses into a colour filter. If one side of the face is genuinely in shadow and the other genuinely lit, the split reads as inevitable rather than applied.

Aim for roughly a 4:1 to 6:1 ratio. Deep, but not so deep the shadow side loses all detail — it still has to hold up under a cold grade.

## Framing

- **Primary crop: 4:5**, waist-up, square to camera.
- **Also deliver a 16:9 crop from the same frame** for interior page headers.
- Hands visible if it can be done without looking posed. He makes things; hands are relevant.
- Head placed so the vertical centre line of a 4:5 crop falls close to the bridge of the nose. The seam runs there.
- No smile. Not stern either — the register is "mid-sentence", not "portrait of a founder".

## Background

**Neutral mid-grey seamless.**

Not black. The handoff brief specified a black or deep-neutral backdrop, and that is wrong for this design: the right-hand half of the image has to sit convincingly on warm paper (`#FBF8F1`). A black background makes the recto half look like a mistake. Mid-grey grades convincingly in both directions from one file.

## Wardrobe

Plain, dark, no pattern, no visible branding. A knit or a plain shirt. Nothing that dates the picture or competes with the type.

## Delivery

- **Shoot RAW.**
- Deliver **flat and ungraded**, 3000px on the long edge, as high-quality JPEG or 16-bit TIFF.
- Do **not** bake in a grade, a vignette, or a black-and-white conversion. Both grades are applied in-browser via CSS filters from the one file, so a baked grade fights the design.
- Retouching: skin only, minimal. Leave the texture.

## Also useful

- One alternate frame looking off-camera, same lighting, for interior page headers.
- One wider frame with more room around the subject, in case the layout wants air later.

---

## What is on the site right now

`public/media/portrait/hal-interim.webp` — a 4:5 crop of an arm's-length wildcamp selfie. It is a real photograph of Hal rather than a stock stranger, and it is captioned on the page as interim, but it does none of the above: no directional key, busy background, and the frame was never intended as a portrait. It is a placeholder for judging layout, not a candidate.

The commissioned frame drops in at that path and the design needs no other change.
